About Me

The journey so far

I'm Aarav Sharma, a Class 12 student currently in the final stretch of JEE Mains preparation. With 60 days remaining, every hour is intentional — balancing rigorous problem-solving with building small tools that keep me organised and motivated.

Beyond textbooks, I enjoy creating simple web projects and study systems. They help me stay accountable and turn abstract concepts into something I can interact with. Physics and Mathematics remain my strongest subjects; Chemistry is the one I treat with extra respect.

This portfolio is both a personal archive and a quiet reminder that progress compounds — one concept, one mock test, one project at a time.
